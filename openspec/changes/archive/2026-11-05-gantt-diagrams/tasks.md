## Notes from apply

Decisions taken at apply, to be written into `docs/decisions/0066-gantt-charts.md` (task 12.5):

- The open questions took the design's recommended answers (section keyword `section`, status words on the line, inclusive `to`, `today` for Mermaid's default, arrows shown, `from` beside `after` as "the later of", durations `s min h d w`, a `roadmap` template, keys T M V G).
- `corpus/invalid/prop.invalid-value-date.meltemi` is not written: the invalid corpus names a file after the code it produces, and `prop.invalid-value` has its sheet; the date, duration and calendar errors are covered by `packages/model/test/gantt.test.ts`. `gantt.excluded-day` (a warning) has a sheet of its own.
- A written `to` on an excluded day ends the task at the end of the last working day before it (`gantt.excluded-day`, warning). A marker's `at` is never moved or warned about: a marker may mark a weekend.
- A milestone or task start that follows from `after` moves to the next working instant silently; only a written `from` or `at` warns.
- Labels: a label beside a bar keeps 6 units from the bar, and 14 (past the arrow's 8-unit first leg) when an arrow leaves it; a label before a bar stands left of the arrow that enters it. A label never stands across an arrow, a marker's line or the today line when another place is free (the place that runs into least is taken otherwise), so the owner's chart reads clean. With the bundled fonts the label of `flu2` also fits inside its bar, so D19's list of labels after the bars is one shorter.
- Section bands are drawn at 60 % opacity so that the grid and the excluded days under them show; markers are drawn behind the bars, so a bar hides the line it crosses.
- `LayoutOptions.today` is a `YYYY-MM-DD` string; the layout never reads a clock. Without it a chart's `today` line is not drawn and an empty chart shows the four weeks of 1970-01-05.
- Sub-minute ticks default to `%H:%M:%S`; the other default formats are as D9 says.
